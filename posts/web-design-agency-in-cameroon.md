### Mobile-First Design Expertise

In Cameroon, over **75% of web traffic** comes from mobile devices. An agency that still builds desktop-first websites is behind the times. Your website must load perfectly on a 3G connection and look great on a budget Android screen.

### Transparent Pricing and Contracts

Reputable agencies provide written quotes before work begins. They itemise what is included — design, development, hosting, domain, SEO — and what costs extra. If an agency is vague about pricing, that is a red flag.

### SEO Knowledge Built In

Getting a beautiful website means nothing if customers cannot find it. A quality agency includes **on-page SEO** as standard — optimised titles, descriptions, headings, image alt text, and site speed tuning — from day one. [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs) provides the official documentation that separates real SEO practitioners from those guessing.

### Ongoing Support and Maintenance

Websites break. Plugins need updating. Content needs changing. Your agency should offer a support plan so you are never left stranded.

## 7 Questions to Ask a Web Design Agency in Cameroon Before Signing

Before you pay any deposit or sign any agreement, ask these questions:

1. **Can I see websites you have built that are live right now?** — Portfolios of mockups are not enough.
2. **What is included in the price?** — Domain, hosting, SEO, revisions, support.
3. **How do you handle revisions?** — How many are included? What is the process?
4. **Who owns the website once it is live?** — You should always own your own domain and site.
5. **What happens if I need changes after launch?** — Is there a support plan?
6. **How long will the project take?** — Get a realistic timeline in writing.
7. **Do you have experience with businesses in my industry?** — Relevant experience matters.
