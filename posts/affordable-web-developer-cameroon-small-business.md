## What to Expect From a Professional Web Developer in Cameroon

### 1. A Discovery & Strategy Session

Before any design begins, a good developer will take time to understand your business, your customers, and your goals. This is not optional. It is what separates a generic template from a website that actually converts visitors into paying customers.

### 2. Custom Design Built Around Your Brand

Your website should look like *your* business, not a copy of someone else's. A professional developer will ask about your brand colours, logo, tone of voice, and the type of customers you want to attract.

### 3. Mobile-First Development

Over **80% of internet users in Cameroon browse on their phones.** A professional developer builds your site mobile-first, meaning it looks perfect and works smoothly on every screen size, from a budget Android phone to a MacBook.

### 4. Basic SEO Setup

Ranking on Google does not happen by accident. A skilled web developer will set up your page titles, meta descriptions, image alt text, and site speed optimisations so your site has a strong foundation from day one. [Ahrefs' guide to on-page SEO](https://ahrefs.com/blog/on-page-seo/) explains why getting this right at the build stage matters so much.

### 5. Training & Handover

Once your site is live, you should know how to make basic updates. A professional developer walks you through the basics and does not leave you stranded after launch.

## 5 Red Flags to Watch Out For When Hiring a Web Developer in Cameroon

Not every developer who claims to be affordable will deliver real value. Watch out for these warning signs:

1. **No portfolio or past work to show**. If they cannot show you websites they have built, walk away.
2. **Promises that sound too good**. "I will build your site in 24 hours for 20,000 FCFA" always ends in disaster.
3. **No written contract or agreement**. Always get deliverables, timelines, and pricing in writing before paying anything.
4. **They disappear after launch**. Post-launch support matters enormously. Ask upfront what happens if something breaks.
5. **They do not ask about your business**. A developer who jumps straight to design without understanding your goals will build the wrong website.
