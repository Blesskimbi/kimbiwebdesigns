### What to Expect From a Professional Web Developer in Cameroon

#### 1. A Discovery & Strategy Session

Before any design begins, a good web developer will take time to understand your business, your customers, and your goals. This is not optional — it is what separates a generic template from a website that actually converts visitors into paying clients.

#### 2. Custom Design Built Around Your Brand

Your website should look and feel like *your* business, not a copy of someone else's. Expect a developer to ask about your brand colors, logo, tone of voice, and the kind of customers you want to attract.

#### 3. Mobile-First Development

Over 80% of internet users in Cameroon browse on their phones. A professional developer builds your site mobile-first, ensuring it looks great and functions perfectly on every screen size.

#### 4. Basic SEO Setup

Ranking on Google does not happen by accident. A skilled web developer will set up your page titles, meta descriptions, image alt text, and site speed optimizations so your site has a strong foundation from launch day.

#### 5. Training & Handover

Once your site is live, you should know how to make basic updates. A good developer walks you through the basics and does not leave you stranded.

### 5 Red Flags to Watch Out For When Hiring a Web Developer in Cameroon

Not every developer who claims to be affordable will deliver real value. Watch out for these warning signs:

1. **No portfolio or past work to show** — If they cannot show you websites they have built, walk away.
2. **Promises that sound too good** — "I'll build your site in 24 hours for 20,000 FCFA" usually ends in disaster.
3. **No contract or written agreement** — Always get deliverables, timelines, and pricing in writing.
4. **They disappear after launch** — Post-launch support matters. Ask upfront what happens if something breaks.
5. **They don't ask about your business** — A developer who jumps straight to design without understanding your goals will build the wrong website.
